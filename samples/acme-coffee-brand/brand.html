02 · LOGOThe cup.

A cup built from three shapes: a bowl, a ring handle and a saucer bar.

  1. The bowl is a 68-unit rectangle ending in a half circle (radius 34). Sides meet the curve where it is upright.
  2. The handle is a ring, radius 17 with an 8 counter, centred on the rim edge.
  3. The saucer is an 84 × 8 bar with round ends, in teal.
xx

Clear space: the height of the saucer bar × 2 on every side. Minimum size: 16 px on screen, 6 mm in print.

ABOUT ACME COFFEE

Acme Coffee roasts and serves coffee in London. Beans are roasted every Tuesday and served within the week, in a flat white that's ready in two minutes. Acme Coffee is a test brand made to check the b-brand-kit skill.
